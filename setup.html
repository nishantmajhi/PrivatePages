Create a 6-digit PIN. You'll use it to lock and unlock sites.

PrivatePages doesn't link to email or any account, so this recovery key is the only way back in if you forget your PIN. Save it somewhere safe (a password manager is ideal). It will not be shown again.

You're all set. Click the PrivatePages icon in your toolbar to lock the site you're on, or open Settings to manage your list.
